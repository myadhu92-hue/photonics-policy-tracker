Photonics is technology that makes, guides and detects light: lasers, fibre-optic links, optical chips and sensors. It carries data-centre and telecom traffic, makes advanced chipmaking possible and is at the heart of LiDAR and some quantum computers. This tracker lists what governments, and the groups advising them, have done about it, with a link to the source for every entry.

Compiler's reading

Since 2025, governments have begun to name photonics in its own right: in the EU's proposed Chips Act 2.0, the US national-security technology strategy, advice to the UK Prime Minister, Taiwan's “economic shield” priorities and India's Semicon 2.0 incentives. US measures are also moving from raw materials such as gallium towards finished optical products, with a bill on Chinese LiDAR and a reported plan to bar new Chinese optical transceivers.

Scope. National and EU-level action in North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific; selective, not exhaustive. Not covered: the Middle East, Latin America, Africa and multilateral bodies. Coverage is thinner for smaller European countries and China's provincial plans.

          Who made this

          An independent personal project, not affiliated with, funded by or endorsed by any employer or organisation. It was researched with AI assistance (Claude), and each entry was checked against its linked source on the date shown with it. It is selective, not exhaustive, and it is not legal, trade-compliance or investment advice: check the official source before relying on a status, especially for export controls.

          What counts

          Strategies, laws and bills, public funding, export, import and procurement controls, public programmes and infrastructure, and published advice, from January 2020, at national or EU level. Broader semiconductor, quantum or critical-materials measures are included only if their official text names photonics, optics, lasers or LiDAR, photonic chips (PICs), indium phosphide, gallium or germanium. — entries that were checked and failed this test are kept in the data file, marked out of scope, and left out of the page, the charts and the download.

          Advice and advocacy entries are included when an industry association, professional body, research or innovation centre, expert network convened for government, advisory council or think tank publishes a specific ask on photonics policy at national or EU level. The same rule applies to every group. They are drawn as a grey cross because they are not government action.

          Reported, not confirmed
          Plans known only from press reports or leaked drafts.
          Paused or temporary
          Measures suspended or in force only until a set date.
          Advice or advocacy
          Recommendations from outside government.
          Withdrawn
          Entries found to be wrong; they stay visible, marked, and the change log says why.

            Glossary

            1260H list
            The Pentagon's list of Chinese military companies; from June 2026 the Defense Department may not contract directly with listed firms.
            CHIPS
            The US CHIPS and Science Act of 2022 and the funding programmes it created.
            LOI and PMT
            Letter of intent and preliminary memorandum of terms: CHIPS awards agreed in principle but not yet final.
            NDAA
            National Defense Authorization Act, the annual US defence law.
            IPCEI
            Important Project of Common European Interest: state aid the EU approves for large cross-border projects.
            Chips JU
            The Chips Joint Undertaking, the EU body that funds chip pilot lines such as PIXEurope.
            CPO
            Co-packaged optics: optical links built into the same package as a processor or switch chip.
            PIC
            Photonic integrated circuit: a chip that routes and processes light.
            InP
            Indium phosphide, the main material for lasers on optical-network chips.
            Ga, Ge, Sb
            Gallium, germanium and antimony, metals used in optoelectronics, infrared optics and fibre.
            LiDAR
            Laser-based distance sensing, used in cars, robots and mapping.
            DUV, EUV
            Deep and extreme ultraviolet lithography, the light-based tools that print chips.
            LOI, PMT
            Letter of intent and preliminary memorandum of terms: non-binding steps before a final US CHIPS award.
            CHIPS
            The US CHIPS and Science Act of 2022 and the programmes it funds.
            1260H list
            The US Defense Department's list of Chinese military companies.
            NDAA
            The annual US National Defense Authorization Act.
            Section 232
            US law that lets the President restrict imports on national-security grounds.
            Chips JU
            The EU's Chips Joint Undertaking, which funds pilot lines.
            IPCEI
            Important Project of Common European Interest: EU-approved state aid across several countries.
            PERTE
            Spain's strategic projects for economic recovery and transformation.
            Horizon Europe, FP10
            The EU's research programme for 2021–27 and its planned successor.
            MOFCOM, MIIT, NDRC
            China's commerce ministry, industry ministry and planning commission.
            METI, NEDO
            Japan's industry ministry and its research-funding agency.
